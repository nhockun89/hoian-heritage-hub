The Taste
of Antiquity

In Hoi An, history is not just preserved in golden walls and silk lanterns; it is served daily in steaming bowls of broth and crisped baguettes. Discover the centuries-old recipes that define the Ancient Town.

The Fundamentals

Street Food Staples

Wander the narrow alleys to find these iconic dishes, where recipes are fiercely guarded family secrets passed down through generations.

Cao Lầu

The soul of Hoi An in a bowl. Authentic Cao Lau can only be made with water drawn from the ancient Ba Le well, mixed with ash from the Cham Islands.

Bánh Mì

A symphony of textures. Crusty baguette, rich paté, savory meats, and sharp pickled vegetables.

Evening Ambiance

Riverside Elegance

As dusk falls and the lanterns are lit, the banks of the Thu Bon River transform into a culinary stage. Experience elevated local cuisine in heritage merchant houses turned fine dining establishments, where the cool river breeze accompanies every course.
