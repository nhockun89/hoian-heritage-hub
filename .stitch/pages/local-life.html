Lunar Traditions

The Full Moon Lantern Festival

Every 14th day of the lunar month, the Ancient Town extinguishes its electric lights. The streets are transformed into a luminous tapestry of silk lanterns, incense smoke, and paper lotus flowers floating down the Thu Bon river.

Sacred Offerings
